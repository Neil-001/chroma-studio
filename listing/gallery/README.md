# Gallery

Screenshots for the galleries on Modrinth and CurseForge, with the title and description for each. The `Listing` workflow uploads them to Modrinth in this order, reading the titles and descriptions from this file; on CurseForge upload them by hand. Titles fit Modrinth's limit of 64 characters and descriptions its 255.

All nine are 1920 by 1080, taken in Minecraft 26.3 at GUI scale 2 with the default textures. The ninth uses Axiom 6.1.5 on Fabric.

## 1. Match a colour

`01-match.jpg`

Blossom pink in a cherry grove at sunset, and the closest blocks, nearest first. Each row has its distance, kind, a badge for how busy the texture is, and the texture as it looks from a distance.

## 2. Match a block

`02-material-set.jpg`

Pale oak planks as the target, in a pale garden at dusk. A row opens to the other blocks of its material, here the logs, wood, slab, stairs, signs, fence and trapdoor, and one click makes them a palette.

## 3. Gradient

`03-gradient.jpg`

Sixteen steps of Nether fire, from near black through crimson and orange to gold. Click a step for its alternatives; here resin bricks replace lava at step 9.

## 4. Palettes

`04-palettes.jpg`

A palette captured from an End city, then given amethyst, crying obsidian and froglight accents. Every block has a weight and a share. The toolbar generates, captures, analyses, exports and fills your hotbar.

## 5. Diorama

`05-diorama.jpg`

The End city palette as a wall of blocks in 3D, mixed by its weights, with each block's share beside it. Drag to turn it, reroll the pattern, and export the wall you see as a schematic.

## 6. Analyse

`06-analyse.jpg`

Analyse says what a palette lacks, here a step between its dark and light blocks and an accent that blends in. Find blocks opens Match on a colour that would fill the gap.

## 7. Explore

`07-explore.jpg`

Every block as a dot, by hue and lightness. Scroll to zoom and click a dot to match it. The outlined dots are the blocks of the End city palette.

## 8. In the world

`08-eyedropper.jpg`

Press X on a block to read its colour, here a giant mushroom's stem. The readout shows the block, its face and a swatch, and copies the hex. The hotbar holds a Nether fire gradient.

## 9. Axiom's editor

`09-axiom.jpg`

With Axiom on Fabric, the Chroma palette brush recolours a village house with the End city palette and keeps its shading. Each stroke is one entry in Axiom's history.
