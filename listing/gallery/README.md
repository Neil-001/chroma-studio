# Gallery

Screenshots for the galleries on Modrinth and CurseForge, with the title and description to enter for each. Upload them by hand, in this order. The descriptions fit Modrinth's limit of 255 characters.

All eight are 1920 by 1080, taken in Minecraft 26.3 at GUI scale 2 with the default textures.

## 1. Match a colour

`01-match.jpg`

Pick a colour and get the closest blocks, nearest first. Each row has its distance, kind, a badge for how busy the texture is, and the texture as it looks from a distance.

## 2. Match a block

`02-material-set.jpg`

Cherry planks as the target. A row opens to the other blocks of its material, here the log, slab, stairs, signs, fence and trapdoor, and one click makes them a palette.

## 3. Gradient

`03-gradient.jpg`

Sixteen steps from dark blue through red to sandstone. The mod picks a different block for every step so the whole run stays as close to the colours as it can.

## 4. Palettes

`04-palettes.jpg`

The Medieval preset, one of ten that come with the mod. Every block has a weight. The toolbar generates, captures, analyses, exports and fills your hotbar.

## 5. Diorama

`05-diorama.jpg`

A palette as a wall of blocks in 3D, mixed by its weights. Drag to turn it, reroll the pattern, and export the wall you see as a schematic.

## 6. Analyse

`06-analyse.jpg`

Analyse says what a palette lacks, such as a dark shade or an accent. Fix opens Match on a colour that would fill the gap.

## 7. Explore

`07-explore.jpg`

Every block as a dot, by hue and lightness. Scroll to zoom and click a dot to match it. The outlined dots are the blocks of the Medieval palette.

## 8. In the world

`08-eyedropper.jpg`

Press X on a block to read its colour, here a grass block's top with the plains tint. Above the hotbar, the names of the nine gradient blocks just put there.
